Writing, literature & specialist models
Manuscript stages need the pinned PaperOrchestra installation, writer credentials and TeX. Check setup reports missing prerequisites. Follow docs/paper-orchestra.md in your installation, then configure paper_orchestra in Advanced JSON. Additional role providers, panel models, token prices and literature sources are available there. Google routing and optional Laya advice are in Model access. These configuration controls do not install or call services.
